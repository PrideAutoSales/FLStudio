# Cruz Synth

A wavetable synthesizer instrument (VST3 + AU) for FL Studio on Mac.

- **Two wavetable oscillators** with up to 16-voice unison (detune, blend, width), phase/random phase,
  and 11 warp modes: Sync, Bend +/-, Asym, PWM, Mirror, Flip, Quantize, and FM/AM/RM from the other oscillator.
  Each frame is stored band-limited per octave, so high notes stay clean.
- **20 original factory wavetables**, generated in code, including talking growl, metal FM, squashed saw,
  comb saw, bit grit and chord stack tables for bass-music and EDM sounds. Drop any WAV on the wave display to load your own.
- **Wavetable editor**: draw frames, edit harmonics, add/delete frames, crossfade or spectral fill, normalize,
  smooth, invert, reverse, import and export WAV.
- **Sub and noise oscillators** (5 noise types with a color control).
- **Filter** with 12 types: Low 12/24, Ladder, High 12/24, Band 12/24, Notch, Peak, Comb +/-, Vowel, plus drive, mix and keytracking.
- **Modulation**: 3 envelopes (Env 1 is the amp), 4 LFOs with drawable shapes, tempo sync and Free/Retrig/Envelope modes,
  4 macros, velocity, note, mod wheel, aftertouch, pitch bend and per-note random.
  Drag any source onto a knob; 16 routings shown on the MATRIX page.
- **Serum-style layout**: OSC, FX, MATRIX and GLOBAL tabs in that order. The OSC page has sub and noise
  on the left, the two oscillators (with OCT/SEM/FIN/CRS boxes above the wave display), the filter on the right,
  the envelopes and LFOs below with a large graph, voicing beside them, and the four macros along the bottom.
- **FX rack with eleven modules**, in any order: Hyper (up to 7 drifting unison copies, plus Widen),
  Distortion (13 modes, 4x oversampled, with a pre or post filter), Flanger (free or synced), Phaser (2 to 12 stages,
  stereo), Chorus (stereo, with a high cut), Delay (sync, ping-pong, stereo offset, low and high cut in the feedback),
  Reverb (Hall, Plate and Room, with decay, pre-delay and low and high cut), Compressor (single or 3-band with
  upward compression), Multiband (three-band upward and downward squash), EQ (two bands, each shelf, peak or cut)
  and Filter (8 types with drive and stereo spread). The FX page lists the chain on the left and shows the
  switched-on modules in a rack, with live curves for the distortion, EQ and filter. Effect knobs can be
  modulated like any other: drag a macro, LFO or envelope onto them.
- Mono/legato/glide, up to 16 voices, user presets saved to `~/Music/Cruz Synth Presets`.
- **234 factory presets** in eight folders of the preset menu: Bass, Leads, Keys, Pads, Strings, Synths
  (plucks, arps, stabs and chords), Deep House and Minimal. They cover future house and bass house, big-room and
  festival leads, dubstep growls, screeches and wobbles, reese and sub basses, pumping sidechain chords and pads,
  deep house organ basses, dub chords, Rhodes and warm pads, and minimal blips, clicks, rubber basses and echo stabs.
  Every preset is level-matched and checked for clipping. Macro 1 is tone on almost every preset and Macro 2 is
  movement; on the Deep House and Minimal presets Macro 3 opens the reverb and Macro 4 the delay.

The factory bank is generated by [tools/make_presets.py](tools/make_presets.py), which writes
`Source/FactoryPresets.cpp`. Volumes come from `tools/preset_gains.json`, the output of a render test that
plays every preset at full velocity.

Install steps: [INSTALL.md](INSTALL.md).
