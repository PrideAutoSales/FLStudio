CRUZ DRUMS (version 2)
======================

576 original drum one-shots: 8 genres x 6 drum types x 12 samples.
24-bit, 48 kHz WAV, loud and ready to drop in.

Version 2 is built around the hard-knocking, textured sound of the best
hip hop kits instead of classic drum machines. Every hit is layered from
modelled real-world sources: drum heads and snare wires, hands clapping,
fists on tables, cardboard boxes, mouths (beatbox), bottles, cans, keys,
paper and shakers. Then it goes through a "knock" chain: a console channel
driven into soft clipping, distortion, EQ reshaping, distortion again,
compression, a small real room, and for some genres sampler crunch, tape
and pitch-down resampling. Everything is synthesized from scratch, nothing
is taken from other packs or records, so the sounds are yours to use.

Folders:  Genre / Kicks, Snares, Claps, Rims, Hats, Percs
Genres:   House, Hip Hop, Trap, Boom Bap, Pop, Disco, Funk, Drum and Bass

File names say what the sound is made of and how it sounds, for example
  "Hip Hop Snare 11 - Table Slam Long Roomy.wav"
  "Trap Kick 05 - 808 Knocky Boomy (F).wav"
The letter in brackets is the note the sample is tuned to.

Every sample is trimmed, peaks at -0.3 dB true peak, has no DC offset and
fades out cleanly. For each folder 22 versions were made, any that failed
the checks (clipping, clicks, stray noise at the end, weak hits) were
thrown out, and the 12 most different from each other were kept.

Adding the pack to FL Studio:
  Options > File settings > click an empty slot under
  "Browser extra search folders" > pick the "Cruz Drums" folder.
