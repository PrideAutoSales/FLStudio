CRUZ DRUMS
==========

576 original drum one-shots: 8 genres x 6 drum types x 12 samples.
24-bit, 48 kHz WAV. Every sound was synthesized from scratch (oscillators,
noise, modelled wood/skin/metal and generated rooms). Nothing is sampled
from other packs or records, so they are yours to use in any track.

Folders:  Genre / Kicks, Snares, Claps, Rims, Hats, Percs
Genres:   House, Hip Hop, Trap, Boom Bap, Pop, Disco, Funk, Drum and Bass

File names describe the sound, for example
  "Trap Kick 05 - 808 Boomy Warm Clicky (F).wav"
The letter in brackets is the note the sample is tuned to, so 808s and
tonal percs drop straight into your track's key.

Every sample is trimmed (starts on the hit, no silent tail), peaks at
-0.3 dB true peak, has no DC offset and fades out cleanly. For each
folder 22 versions were made and the 12 most distinct were kept.

Adding the pack to FL Studio:
  Options > File settings > click an empty slot under
  "Browser extra search folders" > pick the "Cruz Drums" folder.
  It then shows up in the Browser on the left.
